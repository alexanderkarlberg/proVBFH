The (2,0) + (0,2) part works and is cheap

The NLO correction of the radiating line (one loop + I + K + P, and H+4j real minus Catani–Seymour dipoles, all projected to the VBF Born) now integrates stably. It does not depend on the technical cutoff. At a cutoff of 10−4 it reaches 1% of NLO per bin in the VBF distributions with 14–51 CPU-hours, so it fits on the thservs.

Problems found and fixed on the way

bug, mine

Line 1's real used line 2's kinematics

The four-parton point lives in module state, and the driver set up both lines before evaluating either. Found in the first 40k-point test (Σ|w| = 494 ± 242 pb, then 134 ± 13 after the fix). No earlier result used this path.

bug, mine

Real evaluated outside the generator's support

Points far beyond the cutoff (sa1/Q2 = 2.5×10−11) were reached by the wrong sampling path with a tiny density: weights up to 1013. They ruined the VEGAS grids of the first cutoff study, which I aborted. Fix: a consistent technical cut that drops the whole four-parton point if any dipole map is closer to its singular limit than the cutoff.

understood

Remaining variance: double-unresolved corners

In soft-collinear corners a final–final dipole can leave the merged gluon far softer than the real pair, so the dipole exceeds the real by up to 300×. This is the known behaviour of CS dipoles there. It cancels against the Born projection and sets the variance. My first guess (spin correlations) was wrong: replacing them by their azimuthal average changed the dipole by 2%.

Also settled this week

  • T-odd term: VBFNLO is right. An independent unitarity calculation agrees with it to five digits. The difference to NNLOJET was my harness giving NNLOJET the line flavours in the wrong order; with the right order they agree to 2×10−12.
  • nf = 4 in ffunc: an inconsistency introduced in proVBFH's own copy. The public POWHEG code has nf = 4 in both places, which is correct.
  • Public POWHEG VBF_HJJJ: the pair-type swap and the missing initial-state FKS region are both present and demonstrated on the public build (bug report draft sent to you separately).

(B1, R2): the same with 1 ↔ 2
(R1, R2): H4 f1(ξ1) f2(ξ2)

  • The three-parton points of stage 1 are reused. The double-dipole term lies at the Born and cancels with its own projection.
