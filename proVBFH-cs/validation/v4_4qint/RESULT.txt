V4 (13 Oct 2026): identical-quark interference in the four-quark reals (cs_4qint)

WHAT
  The four-quark real (H + 6 quarks, (2,0) and (0,2) parts) read qqh4q's index 0
  (|normal|^2) only. The flipped diagrams (lines 1-6 and 5-2 instead of 1-2 and
  5-6, qqh4q's labels) are the normal ones of partner entries of the list, so
  only the interference I = 2 colint Re(M_N M_F^*) (colint = -2, colsq = 6,
  Fermi sign -1) was missing. cs_4qint 1 (default) adds I/2 per entry whose flip
  is physical (the partner carries the other half): qqh4q with lsymmcontrib =
  .false., index 1 = N + I/2.
  - src/compreal_hqqqq_new.f: flip physical iff the pair flavour equals the
    flavour of a leg of the radiating line (tags: line = tag_type, no pair flag);
    NC: the line flavour, CC: either end of the W line. This guard replaces
    qqh4q's missing flavour guard (NC upper line) and its type-only flavours (no
    u-c interference). CC: index 3 = the pair type (2 - ftype(7)); the caller
    reads only the W-on-12/34 slots (flav1 /= flav3), so qqh4q's charge-violating
    flip for flav1 = flav3 /= flav5, fsign = -1 (W on 16/36) is never used.
    cs_4qint 0: as before; cs_4qint 2: I/2 alone (0 for the other entries).
  - src/qqh4q_me_new.f (copy of proVBFH's): NC lower-line interference used the
    upper-line amplitude mat(...,1) with matflip(...,2); now mat(...,2).
  - cs_main.f90, cs_exclusive.f90, cs_nlo2.f90: cs_4qint 2 = exclusive part made
    of the four-parton interference events and their Born counterevents only
    (no dipoles, no other contributions; groups without interference skipped).

  I is integrable everywhere (collinear: s^-1/2, dPS ~ ds; soft quark: E^-1, dPS
  ~ E dE; N and F are each singular in different invariants: g -> q qbar of the
  normal pair vs of the flipped pair, q -> q of the incoming leg of either), so
  real - dipoles stays integrable with I added and no dipole is missing (the
  same holds in Catani-Seymour in general: identical-quark interference has no
  single-unresolved singularity). Its triple-collinear (double-unresolved)
  behaviour is that of the Born-level projection, handled by P2B.

INCLUSIVE PART: hoppet's NNLO DIS coefficient functions (param-coefs, van Neerven
and Vogt, from Zijlstra and van Neerven) are C2NN2A = C2NSP + C2NSN (NS+,
e.m./NC F2) and C2NC2A = C2NSP - C2NSN (NS-, CC), likewise for FL and F3; the
C2NSN part is the identical-quark interference of q V* -> q q qbar. So the
inclusive part already contains it, for NC and CC. Before the fix the exclusive
part lacked it, i.e. the P2B result missed its cut migration
(int I/2 [O(Phi_4) - O(Phi_B)]); with cs_4qint 1 the two parts are consistent
and the inclusive side needs no change.
